ETSI 5 ETSI TS 103 169 V1.1.1 (2011-09) 1 Scope Recent work in the ETSI Technical Committee Rail Telecommunications (TCRT) has uncovered implementation options in the ETSI standards for VGCS calls which must be clarified by the Railways in order to ensure interoperability between GSM-R networks across Europe.
