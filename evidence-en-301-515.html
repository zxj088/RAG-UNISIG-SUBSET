The present document is applicable to GSM communication systems embraced by the European Council Directives 96/48/EC [1] and 2001/16/EC [2] on the interoperability of the trans-European high-speed and conventional rail system. 2 References The following documents contain provisions which, through reference in this text, constitute provisions of the present document. • References are either specific (identified by date of publication and/or edition number or version number) or non-specific. • For a specific reference, subsequent revisions do not apply. • For a non-specific reference, the latest version applies.
