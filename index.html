How to use this site.

1

Ask

Enter an English question, document reference or exact clause number. All published evidence indexes load automatically.

2

Review

Read direct evidence separately from supporting clause context. The site does not invent or silently paraphrase requirements.

3

Verify

Open the cited ERA-hosted PDF page and confirm wording, tables, figures and surrounding clauses.

Coverage: Search spans 20,049 evidence blocks from 84 evidence-bearing sources. Three additional reserved entries contain no extractable clauses and are identified as source metadata only. For third-party or rights-sensitive material, results provide bounded excerpts and citations rather than complete documents.
