How to use this site.

1

Ask

Enter an English question or exact clause number. SUBSET-098 is searchable now and loads automatically.

2

Review

Read direct evidence separately from supporting clause context. The site does not invent or silently paraphrase requirements.

3

Verify

Open the cited ERA-hosted PDF page and confirm wording, tables, figures and surrounding clauses.

Why only one searchable document? The archive contains material from different rightsholders and extraction quality varies. A document becomes searchable only after its source, version, reuse status and clause/page mapping pass review. Catalogue-only documents still link to the authoritative ERA source.
