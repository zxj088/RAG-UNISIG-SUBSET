Sources & reuse

What this independent project publishes, and what it does not claim.

Source and attribution

Catalogue metadata and the currently searchable SUBSET-098 material originate from the European Union Agency for Railways archive, Set of Specifications 3 — ETCS Baseline 3 Release 2 and GSM-R Baseline 1. Each result identifies the document, version, clause, PDF page and ERA-hosted source.

Independent processing

Source documents may be converted, extracted, segmented and indexed by automated software. The resulting evidence blocks are not official ERA representations. Extraction can lose formatting, tables, diagrams or context; the ERA-hosted PDF remains authoritative.

Third-party material

The ERA archive contains documents that may identify other authors or rightsholders, including ETSI, UIC and industry organisations. Public availability on the ERA website is not treated here as blanket permission to republish every third-party work. Documents with unclear or restrictive notices remain catalogue-only until their reuse basis is established.

No endorsement or official status

This is a non-commercial, independent project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by or maintained by ERA, the European Union, UNISIG, ETSI, UIC or any employer. Their names are used only to identify sources and specification documents. No institutional logos are used.

Safety and accuracy

Search results are navigation aids, not legal, safety, compliance or engineering conclusions. Never rely on extracted text alone for a safety-related decision. Verify the complete clause, surrounding context, tables and figures in the authoritative document.

Privacy and cost

The search runs locally in the browser without an AI model, user account, analytics tracker, external API or application server. The static site is hosted on GitHub Pages.

Rights concern

If you are a rightsholder and believe material is presented incorrectly or should not be available, open an issue in the public repository identifying the document and relevant right.
