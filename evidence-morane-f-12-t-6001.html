SCOPE This Functional Interface Specification (FIS) specifies the GSM-R interface requirements involved in the realisation of the GSM-R feature Location dependent addressing. The called party on the fixed network side is an ISDN party or a non-ISDN party and is called by a specific Short Code, which identifies its function (for instance primary train controller, electricity supply controller, secondary controller). 2.
